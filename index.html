Elementary Armageddon – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Elementary Armageddon ("the game") is made by Pavlos Michalopoulos. This policy explains what happens to your data when you play.

The short version

The game does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no tracking, and it works fully offline.

What is stored on your device

The game saves your progress (level, coins, upgrades, hats) and your settings (sound, vibration, tips, language, graphics quality) locally on your phone. This information never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Permissions

The game uses vibration for haptic feedback (you can turn it off in the menu). It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Third parties

The game does not include third-party advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services. If you download it from Google Play, Google's own privacy policy applies to the store itself.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this (for example by adding online features), this page will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Questions? Email pmichalopoulos@outlook.com.

Πολιτική Απορρήτου (Ελληνικά)

Δεν έχει διαφημίσεις, στατιστικά χρήσης, λογαριασμούς ή παρακολούθηση, και παίζει χωρίς σύνδεση στο ίντερνετ. Η πρόοδος και οι ρυθμίσεις σου αποθηκεύονται μόνο στο κινητό σου και σβήνονται αν απεγκαταστήσεις το παιχνίδι. Για ερωτήσεις: pmichalopoulos@outlook.com.
